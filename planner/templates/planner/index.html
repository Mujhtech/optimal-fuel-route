USA ROUTE PLANNING · 500 MILE RANGE · 10 MPG

Find a cheaper place
to fill up along your route.

This Django API returns a driving route, fuel purchases, total cost, GeoJSON, and an interactive map. OpenAPI specification · Data status

The vehicle starts with 50 gallons. Total cost values the fuel consumed, including starting fuel. Its price uses the nearest listed station quote unless you send initial_fuel_price_per_gallon. Purchases along the route are reported separately.

POST /api/route/
Content-Type: application/json
